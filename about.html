Introduction to Delhi

Delhi is the capital of India and one of its oldest cities. It is famous for its historical monuments, rich culture, traditions and delicious food.

History of Delhi

Delhi has witnessed the rule of several dynasties and empires. The Mughals, Delhi Sultanate and British rulers have all contributed to its history and architecture.

Culture of Delhi

Delhi is known for its diverse culture, festivals, languages, markets and historical monuments. People from different parts of India live here.
